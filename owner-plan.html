診斷插座位置（本車）Diagnostic port location (this car)

2014 年 8 月後生產的右駕 Model S，診斷插座在中控台、觸控螢幕下方的儲物格後面（2014 年 8 月前的右駕車則在右側儀錶板側蓋後）。它不是標準 OBD-II 插座，而是 12 針 TE Connectivity 173851-2 連接器 — 所以第三方工具必須配一條「12 針轉 16 針 OBD-II」的特斯拉專用轉接線，才讀得到內部 CAN 匯流排。車上原有的 OBD-II 位置只提供電壓及 VIN，讀不到電芯數據。
On RHD Model S built after August 2014, the diagnostic connector sits in the centre console, behind the cubby beneath the main touchscreen (pre-Aug 2014 RHD cars have it behind the right instrument-panel side cover). It is not a standard OBD-II socket — it is a 12-pin TE Connectivity 173851-2 connector, which is why third-party tools need a Tesla-specific 12-pin-to-16-pin OBD-II harness to reach the internal CAN bus. The car's nominal OBD-II position carries only voltage and VIN; no cell data.

03 — 「BMS 逐步收窄可用範圍」— 在哪裡看得到03 — "BMS Progressively Restricting Range" — Where You Actually See It

分辨「BMS 限制」與「自然衰減」telling a BMS restriction apart from ordinary degradation

關鍵分辨方法The key distinction

自然衰減是一條緩慢下斜的直線；BMS 限制是一級台階。電池自然老化每年只跌幾個百分點，曲線平順；BMS 主動收窄可用範圍時，數字會在某個事件（例如一次維修、一次深放電、一次故障碼）之後突然跌一級然後停住，而且通常伴隨儀錶板警示或 BMS_u018／u029 一類代碼。

所以看的是曲線形狀，不是單一個數字。單次讀數告訴你不了什麼；連續 6 至 12 個月、每月同一條件下的紀錄，才分得出是老化還是限制。本文件第 06 節附有可列印的紀錄表。
Ordinary degradation is a gentle downward slope; a BMS restriction is a step. Natural ageing loses a few percent a year along a smooth curve. When the BMS actively restricts usable range, the number drops abruptly and then plateaus — tied to a specific event such as a repair, a deep discharge or a fault code — and it is usually accompanied by a dashboard alert or a code in the BMS_u018 / u029 family.

So what you read is the shape of the curve, not any single number. One reading tells you almost nothing; six to twelve months of same-conditions monthly readings tell you whether this is ageing or restriction. Section 06 of this document is a printable log sheet for exactly that.

為何本車的里程類項目來得特別快Why the km-based items come round so fast on this car

香港私家車年均里程一般遠低於本車。此車以約 29,200 km／年行駛，凡以里程計算的保養項目（輪胎調位、迫力檢查、輪胎更換）一年會來三次以上，而以年份計算的項目（迫力油、車廂濾網）則維持不變。把兩者混在同一張「每年保養」清單上，是高里程車最常見的保養漏洞。
This car runs far above the typical Hong Kong private-car mileage. At ≈29,200 km/yr, anything measured in kilometres — tyre rotation, brake inspection, tyre replacement — falls due more than three times a year, while year-based items such as brake fluid and cabin filter do not move. Lumping both onto one "annual service" list is the most common maintenance gap on high-mileage cars.
