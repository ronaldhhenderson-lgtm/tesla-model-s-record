怎樣讀這張圖How to read this chart

每一行代表一個部件。橫棒由「裝上」畫到「換出」，右邊寫出實際用了多久（年 ＋ 公里）。箭頭 ▶ 表示該件仍在車上。◆ 及虛線＝ 預期壽命或保用屆滿的日期；灰色區塊＝ 預期壽命有上下限時的區間。橫棒越過灰區或虛線，代表該件已用超過預期；橫棒遠遠短過灰區，代表早夭。
保用期 ≠ 壽命。灰色方旗 ▮ 標示的是保用屆滿日，屬商業條款，與該件還能用多久無關；只有 ◆ 及灰色區塊才是壽命估算。翻新後驅動總成即為明顯例子：保用 2028-10 屆滿（里程額度約 2027-11 更早到），但按同一業界估算，其預期壽命要到 2029-12 至 2035 年才用完 — 保用結束時，它才用了大約 18% 的壽命。
基準來源：LDU 之 150,000–300,000 km 為維修業估算，按本車年均 29,200 km 換算成年份，同樣套用於翻新件（由 2024-10 裝上起算）；12V 電池 2–4 年為車主社群經驗值。兩者皆非原廠公佈之壽命規格。高壓電池組並無可靠公開壽命基準，故只標示保用屆滿日，不作壽命估算 — 其實際狀況須靠量度（見下方說明）。
Each row is one part. The bar runs from the date it was fitted to the date it was removed, with the life it actually achieved (years + km) on the right. An arrow ▶ means the part is still on the car. ◆ and the dashed line mark the expected-life or warranty end date; the grey block is the expected-life band where the benchmark is a range. A bar running past the grey block or the dashed line means the part outlived expectation; a bar falling far short of it means premature failure.
A warranty is not a life expectancy. The grey flag ▮ marks the warranty end date — a commercial term that says nothing about how much longer the part will run. Only ◆ and the grey blocks are life estimates. The reman drive unit is the clearest case: cover ends Oct 2028 (and the mileage cap bites around Nov 2027, earlier still), but on the same industry estimate its expected life does not run out until somewhere between Dec 2029 and 2035 — when the warranty expires it will have used only about 18% of its life.
Benchmark sources: the 150,000–300,000 km LDU band is a repair-industry estimate, converted to years at this car's 29,200 km/yr and applied to the reman unit from its Oct 2024 install. The 2–4 year 12 V figure is owner-community experience. Neither is an OEM life specification. The HV battery has no reliable published life benchmark, so only its warranty end is marked and no life estimate is drawn — its real condition has to be measured (see the panel below).

高壓電池組維修後的壽命 — 為何無法給出一個數字Life expectancy of the HV pack after the repair — why no number can be given

2026-05 於中山所做的工序為：修復模組 1 個、修復採集板 3 個、全數模組絕緣處理、整包均衡、透氣孔升級、氣密性測試。

整包均衡只會拉平水位，不會告訴你水缸有多大。Tesla Model S 用的是被動式（電阻放電）均衡，只能把各組電芯的電壓／電量狀態拉到一致；它無法修正各模組之間可用容量或內阻的差異。整組電池是串聯的，實際可用容量由最弱的一組決定 — 均衡後車輛顯示正常，並不代表容量已回復。

本車的容量：未知。檔案內沒有維修後的容量報告、模組電壓分佈或絕緣阻值紀錄。原廠 8 年電池保用已於 2024-03 屆滿，此次維修由非原廠授權維修商執行，不受 Tesla 任何保用。經查證，業界並無「模組級維修後電池組壽命」的公開研究或數據集，僅有零星維修業個案報告，且傾向警示而非樂觀 — 已知的風險模式是：修復模組與其餘 15 個老化模組容量不一致，數月內重新漂移，BMS 逐步收窄可用範圍，最終可能出現無法行駛。

所以壽命不是「估」出來，是「量」出來的。唯一誠實的做法是建立基線並看趨勢：以 ScanMyTesla 或 TM-Spy 讀取各組電芯電壓差（mV）及每模組可用容量 CAC，維修後立即記錄一次，其後每 3 個月重複一次，為期至少一年，重點觀察被修復的那個模組及那 3 塊採集板。看的是漂移速度，不是單次數值。參考：車主社群普遍視 2–6 mV 為健康，老車 20–30 mV 仍屬常見，超過 60 mV 或出現「每週明顯上升」才是警號（此為社群經驗值，非原廠門檻）。若三個月內電壓差穩定不變，這組電池大有機會再撐數年；若持續擴大，就要提早規劃更換或再修。
The May 2026 work in Zhongshan was: one module repaired, three collector boards repaired, insulation treatment on all modules, full-pack balancing, vent upgrade, air-tightness test.

Balancing levels the water, it does not measure the tank. The Model S uses passive (bleed-resistor) balancing, which equalises the voltage / state of charge across cell groups. It cannot correct differences in usable capacity or internal resistance between modules. The pack is a series string, so real usable capacity is set by the weakest group — a balanced pack that reads normal on the dash is not evidence that capacity has been restored.

This pack's capacity: unknown. The file contains no post-repair capacity report, no module voltage spread, no insulation resistance figures. Tesla's 8-year battery warranty lapsed in March 2024, and this repair was done by a non-authorised shop, so it carries no Tesla cover. On checking, there is no published study or dataset on the longevity of module-level-repaired packs — only scattered repair-trade case reports, and the strongest of those is cautionary rather than reassuring. The known risk pattern: the repaired module's capacity does not match the other fifteen aged modules, the pack drifts back out of balance within months, the BMS progressively restricts usable range, and in the worst case the car refuses to drive.

So the life expectancy is measured, not estimated. The only honest approach is to establish a baseline and watch the trend: read cell-group voltage spread (mV) and per-module usable capacity (CAC) with ScanMyTesla or TM-Spy, record it now, and repeat every 3 months for at least a year, paying particular attention to the repaired module and the three repaired collector boards. What matters is the rate of drift, not any single reading. For reference, the owner community generally treats 2–6 mV as healthy and 20–30 mV as unremarkable on an old pack, with concern above roughly 60 mV or on any week-on-week climb — these are community figures, not an OEM threshold. If the spread holds steady over three months, this pack has a fair chance of several more years. If it widens, plan the replacement or re-repair early rather than being caught by it.
