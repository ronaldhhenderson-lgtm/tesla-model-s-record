02 — 磨合期计划 Run-In Plan

大修后首 5,000 公里 First 5,000 km after overhaul
发动机及变速箱大修后，内部零件（活塞环、轴瓦、齿轮）需要一段温和磨合期才能达到设计公差；此阶段亦是磨合金属屑最先进入机油的时候。以下里程节点须逐一完成并记录。After an engine/transmission overhaul, internal parts (piston rings, bearings, gears) need a gentle run-in period to reach design tolerances — this is also when the first metal wear particles enter the oil. The milestones below must be completed and logged in sequence.

适用车型 Applicable variant

Some V8-specific items (e.g. starter-motor labour) are noted individually where materially higher than V6. Diesel (1VD-FTV), hybrid, or North-American 5.7L V8 (3UR-FE) variants have different intervals and are not covered here.
